Investment Thesis

The sample property produces positive leverage at the modeled acquisition terms, with an 8.1% going-in capitalization rate and debt-service coverage above 1.40x. The underwriting indicates adequate Year 1 cash flow while retaining room for rent growth and long-term principal reduction. The primary decision risks are the durability of the $4,750 monthly rent assumption, operating-cost inflation, and the effect of higher exit capitalization rates on resale value.

Illustrative recommendation: pursue further due diligence at or below the modeled $425,000 acquisition price, contingent on verification of market rent, insurance, taxes, property condition and comparable sales support.
