CASH FLOW GUIDE

How to calculate rental property cash flow

Cash flow is the money remaining after property operations and scheduled debt payments.

Work from rent to cash flow

PGI Monthly rent × units × 12EGI PGI − vacancy loss + other incomeNOI EGI − operating expensesCash flow NOI − debt service

Include the full expense budget

Account for property taxes, insurance, management, maintenance, owner-paid utilities, association dues and replacement reserves. Mortgage payments and income taxes are modeled separately from operating expenses.

Stress-test the result

Test lower rent, greater vacancy, unexpected repairs and higher recurring expenses. Review the result with DSCR and cash-on-cash return because a thin positive cash flow can disappear quickly.
