Investment Conclusion

Final reconciliation of income, market rent, financing, valuation, risk, and projected returns.

Taken as a whole, this analysis should be read as a test of whether the property’s income, financing structure and projected resale proceeds adequately support the capital required to acquire and hold the asset. The modeled acquisition price of $425,000 is generally consistent with the central income-supported indication under the selected assumptions. Year 1 NOI is $34,425, producing an 8.10% capitalization rate compared with the selected 8.00% benchmark.

The financing structure materially affects the practical investment experience. Year 1 DSCR is 1.42x and modeled operating cash flow is positive. Scheduled principal reduction contributes to equity accumulation throughout the holding period.

The long-term return is projected above the selected required return, with positive NPV under the sample assumptions. A meaningful portion of the investment case nevertheless depends on appreciation, equity buildup and successful disposition rather than current cash flow alone.

Accordingly, the property presents a supportable conventional rental profile under the selected assumptions, subject to verification of achievable market rent, vacancy, operating expenses, financing terms, property condition, appreciation and selling costs. PropertyThesis is evaluating the investment economics under the stated assumptions; changes in those assumptions can materially alter the conclusion.
