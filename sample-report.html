Sensitivity Considerations

Rent downside

A 10% reduction in achieved rent would materially compress cash flow and debt coverage. The rent assumption should therefore be supported by current comparable leases rather than asking rents alone.

Expense pressure

Insurance or tax increases above the modeled growth rates reduce NOI dollar-for-dollar and should be stress-tested before acquisition.

Interest-rate risk

The sample assumes fixed-rate debt. Alternative financing terms can change both maximum supported price and cash-on-cash performance.

Exit risk

Long-term value depends on future NOI and the exit capitalization rate. Higher terminal cap rates can offset appreciation and operating growth.
