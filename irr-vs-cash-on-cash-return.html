RETURN COMPARISON

IRR vs. cash-on-cash return

Cash-on-cash emphasizes annual cash yield. IRR evaluates the timing of all modeled cash flows across the holding period.

Cash-on-cash return

Annual cash flow divided by initial cash invested. It is intuitive for Year 1 income but excludes unrealized appreciation, principal reduction and future sale proceeds.

Internal rate of return

IRR is the discount rate that makes the net present value of modeled cash flows equal zero. It incorporates acquisition equity, annual flows and net sale proceeds, making exit assumptions especially important.

Use both

A property can have weak current cash yield but a higher projected IRR because of appreciation or sale assumptions. Review both with NPV, cash flow, DSCR and sensitivity analysis.
