RETURN METRIC GUIDE

What is cash-on-cash return?

Cash-on-cash return compares annual property cash flow with the cash equity invested at acquisition.

The formula

Cash-on-cash return Annual before-tax cash flow ÷ initial cash invested

Initial cash can include down payment, closing costs, financing fees and immediate improvements. Use consistent definitions when comparing properties.

What it tells you

The metric emphasizes current cash yield after financing, so loan terms materially affect it. It is useful for comparing Year 1 liquidity and the efficiency of invested equity.

What it leaves out

It excludes future growth, principal reduction, appreciation and sale proceeds. Pair it with IRR, NPV, DSCR and sensitivity analysis.
