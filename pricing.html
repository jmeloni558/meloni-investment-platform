What counts as an analysis?

A charge or monthly allowance is used only when results are first unlocked for a new property. PropertyThesis does not charge you again simply because better information becomes available.

Included: Change rents, expenses, financing, taxes, growth and sale assumptions as often as needed.
Included: Recalculate results, compare scenarios and regenerate PDF or Excel deliverables.
Not counted: The public sample, failed calculations or returning to an already unlocked property.
Counts as new: Unlocking results for a different property or creating a separate copy as a new deal.
PRICING QUESTIONS

Know exactly what you are paying for.

Do purchased property unlocks expire?

No. A $15 property unlock remains attached to that property permanently.

What happens if I cancel a subscription?

Your previously unlocked properties remain available. You simply stop receiving new monthly analysis capacity.

Do unused monthly analyses roll over?

No. Professional 50 capacity resets each billing month. One-time property unlocks do not expire.

Is Unlimited really unlimited?

Yes for normal use by one professional. Automated bulk activity, resale and shared credentials are excluded under reasonable-use protections.

Can I change plans?

You can cancel future renewals through secure billing management. To move to a different plan, cancel your current plan and select the new plan after the current billing period ends.

Will revising a report cost more?

No. Reports and exports can be regenerated whenever an unlocked property's assumptions change.
