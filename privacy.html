PROPERTYTHESIS LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

This policy explains how MELONI REALTY INC, doing business as PropertyThesis ("PropertyThesis," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use propertythesis.com and the PropertyThesis real estate investment analysis service.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, authentication records, and optional profile or branding details you provide.
  • Analysis information: property addresses, investment assumptions, client records you choose to enter, saved analyses, reports, and related files or settings.
  • Transaction information: subscription status, plan, and payment-related identifiers. Payment card information is collected and processed by Stripe and is not stored by PropertyThesis.
  • Technical information: browser and device information, IP address, security and diagnostic events, and local browser storage used to maintain sessions, drafts, and preferences.
  • Third-party property information: listing, address, map, and property data returned by services used at your request.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide, save, and generate analyses and reports; operate subscriptions; retrieve requested property information; prevent abuse; troubleshoot and improve the service; communicate service or account information; and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers to operate important parts of PropertyThesis, including Supabase for authentication and data storage, Stripe for payments, Google for address and map features, OpenAI for optional AI-powered site guidance, and property-data providers for requested searches. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer.

AI-powered site guidance

When you use the PropertyThesis Guide, your messages and limited context about the page you are viewing are sent to our AI service provider to generate a response. Do not enter passwords, payment-card information, Social Security numbers, confidential client information, or other sensitive information in the assistant. AI responses may be inaccurate and are provided only as educational product guidance.

Data choices and retention

You may update certain profile and saved-analysis information inside your account. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. We retain information while needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some backup, security, or transaction records may remain for a limited period after deletion.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including authenticated access and account-specific database controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your password and notify us if you suspect unauthorized account activity.

Children and geographic use

PropertyThesis is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. The service is operated from the United States; information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the revised effective date on this page. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to jamie@propertythesis.com or MELONI REALTY INC, 18012 Loretta Lane, Lutz, FL 33548.
