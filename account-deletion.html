PROPERTYTHESIS ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Request account deletion

PropertyThesis is provided by MELONI REALTY INC.

Email jamie@propertythesis.com with the subject PropertyThesis account deletion. Send the request from your account's email address where possible, and identify the PropertyThesis account you want deleted. Do not send your password or payment-card details. You do not need to install the app or sign in to request deletion.

The email link opens your email app. Review and send the message to submit your request. Opening the link alone does not send a message or delete your account. If the link does not work, compose a message to the address above.

What happens next

We verify control of the account before processing deletion. Download any reports you want to keep before requesting deletion. Account deletion and a refund request are separate; identify any refund question in your message.

Information covered

Your request covers your PropertyThesis login and associated saved workspace, including properties, analyses and versions, scenarios, client information, saved research, report settings and account-owned branding files. We also address any recurring PropertyThesis subscription associated with the account.

Files you exported to your device or shared with others are outside our direct control. Deleting your account does not remove those copies. Remove them from your devices or contact their recipients as appropriate.

Limited retained information

Some accounting, security, dispute or legal records and recovery backups may remain after active account deletion. Our response will explain the applicable retained categories, reasons and expiry dates. See our privacy policy.

Support email is handled manually. Removing a message from our active support folder does not establish that Microsoft 365 has erased all recoverable copies. We have not verified the maximum lifetime of those provider-held email copies.
