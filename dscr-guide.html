FINANCING GUIDE

What is DSCR in real estate?

Debt Service Coverage Ratio compares property NOI with scheduled principal and interest payments.

DSCR formula

DSCR Net Operating Income ÷ annual debt service

A 1.00x DSCR means modeled NOI equals debt service. Above 1.00x indicates a cushion; below it indicates an operating shortfall before other investor cash needs.

Why DSCR changes

Rent, vacancy and expenses affect NOI. Loan amount, interest rate and amortization affect debt service. Test both sides instead of treating one ratio as permanent.

Use it with other metrics

DSCR addresses debt coverage, not total return or market value. Review it with cash flow, cash-on-cash return, cap rate and downside scenarios.
