CAP RATE GUIDE

What is cap rate in real estate?

Cap rate expresses Year 1 net operating income as a percentage of property price or value.

Formula and example

Cap rate NOI ÷ price

$30,000 of NOI on a $500,000 property equals 6.0%. NOI is effective gross income minus operating expenses, before debt service and income taxes.

Interpret it carefully

A higher rate can mean more income relative to price, but may also indicate greater risk. Cap rate excludes financing, growth and sale proceeds, so pair it with DSCR, cash flow, IRR and NPV.
